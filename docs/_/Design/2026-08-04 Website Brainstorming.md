By choosing these occupational qualifications, you are investing in training that is designed by industry, for industry.   WHAT ARE THE ADVANTAGES OF A QCTO CERTIFICATE FOR YOU AS AN EMPLOYER?   Unlike older unit-standard-based training, QCTO certificates focus on “occupational qualifications.” This means the training is split into three distinct modules: knowledge, practical skills, and workplace experience. For you, the employer, this results in:Enhanced competency: Your employees don’t just “know” the theory; they have proven they can apply it in your specific workplace., Streamlined accreditation: The QCTO provides a single, high-standard framework that simplifies the verification of skills., Future-proofing: As legacy SETA qualifications expire, moving your team to QCTO-aligned programmes ensures your B-BBEE points and tax incentives remain secure.QCTO LEARNERSHIPS THAT TRAINING FORCE CAN PROVIDE FOR YOUR TEAM?
