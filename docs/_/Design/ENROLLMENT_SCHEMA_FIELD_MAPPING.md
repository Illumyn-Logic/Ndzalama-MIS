# Enrollment workbook to Prisma field mapping

Source: `new_learner_enrollment_spreadsheets (1).xlsx`, sheet `Learner details`.
The worksheet's lookup lists are reference values, not learner records.

| Workbook column | Prisma destination | Mapping notes |
| --- | --- | --- |

`User.orgEmail` and `Profile.studentNumber` are generated by the LMIS and are
not workbook inputs. The workbook has no NQF-level-attained, employer-name, or
sponsor-reference column; `Profile.nqfLevelAttained`, `Profile.employerName`,
and `Profile.sponsorRef` remain optional rather than being inferred from
unrelated fields. Enrollment, intervention, assessment, and payment records
belong to their respective models, not to the learner profile.
