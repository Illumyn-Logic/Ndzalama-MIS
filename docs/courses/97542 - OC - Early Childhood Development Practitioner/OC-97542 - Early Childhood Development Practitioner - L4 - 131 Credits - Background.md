**SAQA 97542: Occ Cert: Early Childhood Development Practitioner Level 4** Credits: 131 Accreditation: QCTO Duration: 1 Year Qualification: 97542 (Part time)

Occupational Certificate: Early Childhood Development Practitioner qualification is to prepare a learner to operate as an Early Childhood Development Practitioner

**Qualification description**

The purpose of the SAQA Qualification 97542: Occupational Certificate: Early Childhood Development Practitioner qualification is to prepare a learner to operate as an Early Childhood Development Practitioner. An Early Childhood Development Practitioner plans and prepares early childhood activities, facilitates and mediates learning, observes and assesses the progress of children, and reflects on learning in an inclusive, play-based environment to support holistic development of children in different centre-based or non-centre-based settings.

**Detailed Information**

Entry requirements:

-   National Certificate Vocational (NCV) at NQF level 3
-   Any other NQF Level 3 qualification as a minimum.

Who is this training for?

-   People who wish to enter and/or continue learning in the early childhood development field, which include the employed, under-employed but not qualified, and school leavers.
-   Those wishing to gain recognition to practitioners at an entry level.
-   Practitioners to increase their employment prospects within the Department of Basic Education.

This qualification may also provide business opportunities to successful practitioners who may wish to establish early childhood centres and/or sites.

-   Child-Minders
-   Crèche Staff
-   Playgroup and Pre-School Staff
-   Special Education Needs Support Staff
-   Au Pairs
